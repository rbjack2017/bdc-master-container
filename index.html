Response & Messaging Systems Core


      

        Customer Response Handling
        
    
          
  • Match your reply to what the customer gave you.
    • 
          
  • Keep answers helpful, address their exact concern, and end with one clear next step.
    • 
          
  • Use appropriate templates (email, text, VM) based on their tone and urgency.
    • 
        

      


      

        Quick Message Customization Tips
        
    
          
  • Replace placeholders (name, vehicle, timeline) before sending.
    • 
          
  • Remove anything that does not apply to this customer.
    • 
          
  • Ask one main question and end with one clear next step.
    • 
          
  • Match the customer’s tone and keep messages short and easy to answer.
    • 
        

      


      

        Vehicle Sold Redirect Messages
        
    
          
  • Use “Vehicle Sold” text/email to notify the customer their vehicle of interest has sold.
    • 
          
  • Offer similar alternatives and invite them to review options.
    • 
          
  • Keep tone helpful and forward-looking (focus on solutions, not loss).
    • 
        

      


      

        ReplyReady Lead Playbooks
        
    
          
  • Copy-ready lead follow-up systems for dealerships and sales teams.
    • 
          
  • Respond faster, sound more professional, and turn more leads into real conversations.
    • 
          
  • Use structured sequences for New Lead, Price, Trade, Appointment, and Quiet Lead scenarios.
    • 
        

      


      

        Work Emails and Follow-Up Message Playbook
        
    
          
  • Start with the guide and checklist, then choose an email or text based on the customer’s situation.
    • 
          
  • Use response handling and word tracks when a customer replies with a question, objection, or buying signal.
    • 
          
  • Keep follow-up consistent and documented inside your CRM or portal.
    • 
        

      

    


    
    

      
Customer Questions Library Core


      

        1. Discovery – Needs & Preferences
        
    
          
  • What can I help you accomplish today?
    • 
          
  • What are you looking for in your next vehicle?
    • 
          
  • What are your must-have requirements?
    • 
          
  • What will you mainly be using the vehicle for?
    • 
          
  • How many passengers or how much space do you typically need?
    • 
          
  • What features are most important to you?
    • 
          
  • What have you seen so far that you’ve liked or didn’t like?
    • 
          
  • What’s prompting the change right now?
    • 
          
  • If everything went perfectly, what would your next vehicle do differently for you?
    • 
          
  • What do you wish your current vehicle did better?
    • 
          
  • Urgency & Consequence (Subtle)
    • 
          
  • What happens if you don’t make a change right now?
    • 
          
  • Is there anything pushing you to make a decision sooner rather than later?
    • 
        

      


      

        2. Vehicle Fit & Options
        
    
          
  • Have you driven anything you’re considering?
    • 
          
  • Have you seen or experienced this vehicle in person yet?
    • 
          
  • Do you have a specific model, trim, or color in mind?
    • 
          
  • Are you open to similar options if I find something comparable?
    • 
          
  • If the exact vehicle isn’t available, what are you willing to be flexible on: color, mileage, trim, features, or price?
    • 
          
  • Are there any deal-breakers I should avoid when looking at options for you?
    • 
          
  • Which vehicle are you most interested in: year, make, model, or stock number?
    • 
          
  • Would you like to see a Carfax report on this vehicle?
    • 
          
  • What would make you feel confident about owning this vehicle long-term?
    • 
          
  • Is warranty coverage or maintenance history important to you?
    • 
          
  • Do you typically keep vehicles for a few years, or drive them as long as possible?
    • 
          
  • What does a typical day with your vehicle look like?
    • 
          
  • Are you ready to schedule a time to come in and look at our vehicle, or are you looking to get pricing together first?
    • 
          
  • What stood out to you when you saw this vehicle online?
    • 
          
  • Was there anything missing from the listing that you wanted to know?
    • 
          
  • Would photos, a video, or a quick side-by-side comparison help?
    • 
          
  • Competitive Insight
    • 
          
  • What other vehicles are you comparing this to?
    • 
          
  • What stands out to you about those options?
    • 
          
  • How do you think this would fit into your daily routine?
    • 
        

      


      

        3. Budget & Financing
        
    
          
  • Are you planning to pay cash, finance, or lease?
    • 
          
  • Did you have a down payment amount in mind?
    • 
          
  • Would you like to get pre-approved before coming in?
    • 
          
  • What feels like a comfortable investment for you?
    • 
          
  • How do you usually prefer to structure a purchase like this?
    • 
          
  • Do you mind if we talk briefly about budget, so I don’t show you something outside your comfort zone?
    • 
          
  • Have you spent any time looking at pricing or payments yet?
    • 
          
  • Are you looking to purchase or lease?
    • 
          
  • How much would you like to put down, if any?
    • 
          
  • What monthly payment range are you hoping to stay within?
    • 
          
  • Have you already been pre-approved with a lender, or would you like us to help with financing?
    • 
        

      


      

        4. Trade-In (If Applicable)
        
    
          
  • Are you considering trading in your current vehicle?
    • 
          
  • Do you have a vehicle to trade in?
    • 
          
  • About how many miles are on it?
    • 
          
  • Is it paid off or still financed?
    • 
          
  • Would you like us to provide a quick appraisal when you come in?
    • 
        

      


      

        5. Appointment & Next Steps
        
    
          
  • Would you like to schedule a time to stop by and test drive?
    • 
          
  • What day and time works best for you?
    • 
          
  • How would you feel about taking it for a spin today or this evening?
    • 
          
  • Would you like to speak with a product specialist before coming in?
    • 
          
  • Is there anything I can have ready for you before you arrive?
    • 
          
  • How does this option feel compared to what you had in mind?
    • 
          
  • On a scale from 1–10, how close is this to what you’re looking for?
    • 
          
  • Want me to walk you through how this usually goes, so you know what to expect?
    • 
          
  • Typically, we’ll narrow down what fits you best, look at a couple of options, and then, if it makes sense, go over numbers—sound fair?
    • 
          
  • Is there anything about this that doesn’t feel quite right yet?
    • 
          
  • If everything lined up the way you wanted, would this be something you’d move forward with?
    • 
          
  • What would need to happen for you to feel this was worth the trip in?
    • 
          
  • Would you prefer I narrow this down to one best option or send you a few choices?
    • 
        

      


      

        6. Communication & Follow-Up
        
    
          
  • What’s the best way to reach you—text, email, or call?
    • 
          
  • What is your ZIP code?
    • 
          
  • What’s holding you back from coming in to take a look?
    • 
          
  • What would make this a great experience for you?
    • 
          
  • What concerns, if any, do you still have?
    • 
          
  • Is there anything you’re unsure about that I can help clarify?
    • 
          
  • What would you need to see or hear to feel 100% comfortable moving forward?
    • 
          
  • If you leave today and want to think on it, what would the next step look like for you?
    • 
        

      


      

        7. Decision Process
        
    
          
  • Besides yourself, is there anyone else involved in the decision?
    • 
          
  • What does your decision timeline look like?
    • 
          
  • What’s most important to you when choosing where to buy from?
    • 
          
  • What kind of experience are you hoping to have during this process?
    • 
          
  • Are you currently just researching, or are you hoping to make a purchase in the next 30 days?
    • 
        

      


      

        8. Emotional Connection / Personal Context
        
    
          
  • What do you enjoy most about driving?
    • 
          
  • What would make this vehicle feel like the perfect fit for you personally?
    • 
          
  • What kind of vehicle feels most like you?
    • 
          
  • When you think about your next car, what do you want it to say about you?
    • 
          
  • What would make you excited to get in your car every day?
    • 
          
  • What would give you the most peace of mind in your next vehicle?
    • 
          
  • How would having the right vehicle change your day-to-day?
    • 
          
  • What would this make easier for you or your family?
    •

  • What's the best number to reach you at if I find a similar vehicle or a price change you'd want to know about right away?
    • 
          
  • Would it be easier to text you a quick walkaround video of the vehicle? If so, what's the best mobile number?
    • 
          
  • If I locate additional options that fit what you're looking for, what's the best number to send them to?
    • 
          
  • I don't want to fill up your inbox—would you prefer email only, text updates, or a quick phone call when I have important information?
