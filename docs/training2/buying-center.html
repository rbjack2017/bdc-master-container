Buying Center Module

The Buying Center Module teaches reps how to handle acquisition leads, trade‑in inquiries, and customers looking to sell their vehicles. This module ensures compliant communication, accurate information gathering, and smooth handoff to the Buying Center team.

1. Purpose of the Buying Center

  • Acquire high‑quality used inventory
  • Provide customers with fast appraisal options
  • Increase dealership profitability
  • Offer a streamlined selling experience

2. Buying Center Lead Types

  • Trade‑in inquiries
  • Sell‑my‑car leads
  • KBB/Edmunds appraisal leads
  • Service‑to‑sales opportunities

3. Required Information to Gather

Reps must collect accurate vehicle details before sending the lead to the Buying Center team.

  • Year, make, model, trim
  • Mileage
  • VIN (if available)
  • Condition details
  • Photos (if customer is willing)

4. Compliance Rules

Buying Center communication must follow strict compliance guidelines.

  • No quoting values
  • No guarantees (“We will pay…”)
  • No appraisal ranges
  • No conditional promises

5. Approved Buying Center Language

  • “Our Buying Center team can provide an exact value after a quick review.”
  • “Are you able to share the mileage?”
  • “Would you like to upload a few photos?”
  • “I can connect you with our appraisal specialist.”

6. Buying Center Workflow

  • Gather vehicle details
  • Document everything in CRM
  • Send lead to Buying Center team
  • Follow up if customer becomes unresponsive

7. Buying Center Summary

The Buying Center helps the dealership acquire inventory while giving customers a fast, easy selling experience. Reps play a key role in gathering accurate information and maintaining compliance.
