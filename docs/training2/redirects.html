Redirects Module

The Redirects Module teaches reps how to properly guide customers toward alternative options when their vehicle of interest is sold, unavailable, or not a good match. Redirects help maintain engagement and prevent opportunity loss.

1. Purpose of Redirects

  • Keep the customer engaged
  • Offer similar or better options
  • Prevent opportunity drop-off
  • Maintain professionalism and transparency

2. When to Use a Redirect

  • The vehicle has sold
  • The vehicle is no longer available
  • The customer’s needs match a different model better
  • The customer requests alternatives

3. Approved Redirect Language

Redirect messages must be short, helpful, and easy to answer.

  • “Hi {{First Name}}, the {{Vehicle}} has sold, but I can help find similar options.”
  • “Are you open to other colors or trims?”
  • “I have a few similar vehicles — want me to send them?”

4. Redirect Rules

  • Never apologize excessively
  • Never mention internal dealership details
  • Always offer alternatives immediately
  • Keep tone positive and solution-focused

5. Redirect Workflow

  • Send redirect text
  • Send redirect email
  • Provide 2–3 similar options
  • Document everything in CRM

6. Redirect Summary

Redirects help reps maintain momentum, guide customers toward available inventory, and preserve opportunities even when the original vehicle is unavailable.
