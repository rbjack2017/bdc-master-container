Customer Response Systems

General Response Rules
  • Match the customer’s tone.
  • Answer exactly what they asked.
  • Remove anything that doesn’t apply.
  • Ask one clear next-step question.
  • Keep responses short and easy to answer.
Buying Signals
  • “Is it available?” → Confirm + offer appointment.
  • “Can you send pricing?” → Send price + ask budget.
  • “Do you take trades?” → Ask for VIN + miles + photos.
  • “What’s your best price?” → Provide breakdown + ask timeline.
  • “I’m just looking.” → Ask what they’re comparing.
Objection Handling
  • Price too high: “I can show similar options or explain what affects pricing.”
  • Payment too high: “Would you consider different terms or similar models?”
  • Not ready yet: “What timeline works best for you?”
  • Just browsing: “What features matter most to you?”
  • Need to think: “What can I clarify while you’re deciding?”
Redirect Responses 
Hi {{First Name}}, the {{Vehicle}} you asked about has sold, 
but I can help find similar options. 
Are you flexible on color, trim, or mileage?
Price Responses 
Hi {{First Name}}, thanks for asking about pricing on {{Vehicle}}. 
I’ve sent a full breakdown to your email. 
Would you like me to include similar options?
Trade Responses 
Hi {{First Name}}, I can help with your trade-in. 
Could you send the VIN, miles, and a few photos? 
This helps me get you the most accurate value.
Quiet Lead Responses 
Hi {{First Name}}, just checking in — still interested in {{Vehicle}}? 
I’m here if you need anything.
Clarifying Questions
  • “What’s most important to you in your next vehicle?”
  • “Are you comparing a few options?”
  • “Do you have a preferred budget or payment range?”
  • “When are you hoping to make a decision?”
  • “Are you planning to trade a vehicle?”
Follow-Up Routing
  • Hot Lead: Respond within 10 minutes.
  • Warm Lead: Respond within 1 hour.
  • Cold Lead: Respond within 24 hours.
  • Trade Lead: Request VIN + miles + photos.
  • Price Lead: Send price + ask timeline.
  • Sold Lead: Offer alternatives.
Escalation Rules
  • Escalate if customer requests a manager.
  • Escalate if customer is upset or confused.
  • Escalate if customer asks for final numbers.
  • Escalate if customer wants to negotiate.
  • Escalate if customer is ready to buy.
Close-Out Rules
  • Close after 7 days of no response.
  • Send final follow-up before closing.
  • Move to long-term follow-up (30/60/90 days).
  • Document final status clearly.
