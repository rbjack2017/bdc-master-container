Brand Identity System

Brand Colors
  • Ivory — #f7f3eb
  • Gold — #c79a3b
  • Charcoal — #333333
  • Dusty Rose — #d9a5b3
  • Sage — #9bb59b
  • Terracotta — #c46a4a
Typography
  • Primary Font: System UI (clean, modern, accessible)
  • Headings: Bold, minimal, no decorative fonts
  • Body Text: Neutral tone, high readability
  • Spacing: 1.25–1.5 line height
  • Rules: Avoid ALL CAPS except for badges
Voice & Tone
  • Warm, professional, and human
  • Clear and direct — no fluff
  • Empathetic but efficient
  • One main question per message
  • Short sentences, easy to answer
  • Match customer tone (formal, casual, urgent)
Logo Usage
  • Minimum clear space: height of the “B” around all sides
  • Do not stretch, distort, or recolor the logo
  • Use gold or charcoal versions on light backgrounds
  • Use ivory or gold versions on dark backgrounds
  • Do not place logo over busy images
Layout Rules
  • Use consistent spacing between sections
  • Rounded corners for cards and containers
  • Soft shadows for depth
  • Minimal borders — use soft gold or soft charcoal
  • Never overcrowd a section
  • Use collapsible sections for long content
Image Style
  • Warm lighting, natural tones
  • Clean backgrounds
  • No harsh contrast or neon colors
  • Prefer lifestyle photos over stock images
  • Vehicles should be clean, centered, and well-lit
Messaging Identity
  • One clear question per message
  • Short, specific, and helpful
  • Remove anything that doesn’t apply
  • Always offer one simple next step
  • Match customer urgency
  • Use customer name and vehicle
Brand Do & Don’t
  • Do: Keep messages short and human
  • Do: Use brand colors consistently
  • Do: Keep layout clean and minimal
  • Don’t: Use slang or emojis in professional messages
  • Don’t: Overload customers with information
  • Don’t: Use unapproved colors or fonts
