Customer Question Framework

Discovery – Needs & Preferences
  • What can I help you accomplish today?
  • What are you looking for in your next vehicle?
  • What are your must-have requirements?
  • What will you mainly be using the vehicle for?
  • How many passengers or how much space do you typically need?
  • What features are most important to you?
  • What have you seen so far that you’ve liked or didn’t like?
  • What’s prompting the change right now?
  • If everything went perfectly, what would your next vehicle do differently for you?
  • What do you wish your current vehicle did better?
  • What happens if you don’t make a change right now?
  • Is there anything pushing you to make a decision sooner rather than later?
Vehicle Fit & Options
  • Have you driven anything you’re considering?
  • Have you seen or experienced this vehicle in person yet?
  • Do you have a specific model, trim, or color in mind?
  • Are you open to similar options if I find something comparable?
  • If the exact vehicle isn’t available, what are you willing to be flexible on?
  • Are there any deal-breakers I should avoid?
  • Which vehicle are you most interested in?
  • Would you like to see a Carfax report?
  • What would make you feel confident about owning this vehicle long-term?
  • Is warranty coverage or maintenance history important to you?
  • Do you typically keep vehicles for a few years or as long as possible?
  • What does a typical day with your vehicle look like?
  • Would photos, a video, or a side-by-side comparison help?
  • What other vehicles are you comparing this to?
  • What stands out to you about those options?
  • How do you think this would fit into your daily routine?
Budget & Financing
  • Are you planning to pay cash, finance, or lease?
  • Did you have a down payment amount in mind?
  • Would you like to get pre-approved before coming in?
  • What feels like a comfortable investment for you?
  • How do you usually prefer to structure a purchase like this?
  • Do you mind if we talk briefly about budget?
  • Have you spent any time looking at pricing or payments yet?
  • What monthly payment range are you hoping to stay within?
  • Have you already been pre-approved with a lender?
Trade-In
  • Are you considering trading in your current vehicle?
  • Do you have a vehicle to trade in?
  • About how many miles are on it?
  • Is it paid off or still financed?
  • Would you like us to provide a quick appraisal?
Appointment & Next Steps
  • Would you like to schedule a time to stop by and test drive?
  • What day and time works best for you?
  • Would you like to speak with a product specialist before coming in?
  • Is there anything I can have ready for you before you arrive?
  • How does this option feel compared to what you had in mind?
  • On a scale from 1–10, how close is this to what you’re looking for?
  • Want me to walk you through how this usually goes?
  • Is there anything about this that doesn’t feel quite right yet?
  • If everything lined up, would this be something you’d move forward with?
  • Would you prefer one best option or a few choices?
Communication & Follow-Up
  • What’s the best way to reach you — text, email, or call?
  • What’s your ZIP code?
  • What’s holding you back from coming in?
  • What would make this a great experience for you?
  • What concerns, if any, do you still have?
  • Is there anything you’re unsure about?
  • If you leave today and want to think on it, what would the next step look like?
Decision Process
  • Besides yourself, is anyone else involved in the decision?
  • What does your decision timeline look like?
  • What’s most important to you when choosing where to buy from?
  • What kind of experience are you hoping to have?
  • Are you researching or hoping to purchase in the next 30 days?
Emotional Connection / Personal Context
  • What do you enjoy most about driving?
  • What would make this vehicle feel like the perfect fit?
  • What kind of vehicle feels most like you?
  • What do you want your next car to say about you?
  • What would make you excited to get in your car every day?
  • What would give you the most peace of mind?
  • How would having the right vehicle change your day-to-day?
  • What would this make easier for you or your family?
Phone Number Requests
  • What’s the best number to reach you at?
  • Would it be easier to text you a quick walkaround video?
  • If I locate additional options, what’s the best number to send them to?
  • Would you prefer email only, text updates, or a quick call?
