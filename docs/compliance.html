BDC Enterprise Compliance System

Compliance Purpose & Executive Outcome
  • Zero-risk communication environment
  • Legal protection
  • Marketplace protection
  • OEM protection
  • Internal policy protection
  • AI usage protection
  • Outcome: A BDC that is legally protected, audit-ready, and compliance-certified.
Texting Compliance
  • No pricing
  • No payments
  • No VINs
  • No promises
  • No pressure
  • One question only
Email Compliance
  • No payment promises
  • No misleading statements
  • No conditional guarantees
  • No unverifiable claims
  • No pressure language
Marketplace Compliance
  • No pricing in text
  • No payment promises
  • No “guaranteed approval”
  • No “guaranteed value”
  • No misleading availability claims
Fresh Start / Credit Compliance
  • No guaranteed approvals
  • No guaranteed rates
  • No guaranteed terms
  • No guaranteed payments
  • No misleading credit claims
OEM Compliance
  • Follow OEM messaging
  • Follow OEM disclaimers
  • Follow OEM program rules
  • Follow OEM incentive language
Internal Policy Compliance
  • Workflow accuracy
  • Template accuracy
  • Escalation accuracy
  • CRM accuracy
  • Reporting accuracy
AI Compliance
  • AI suggestions must be reviewed
  • AI templates must be approved
  • AI routing must be monitored
  • AI objections must be validated
Compliance Enforcement Structure
  • BDC Rep: Follow templates, workflows, texting rules.
  • Senior Rep: Monitor rep compliance, correct errors.
  • Team Lead: Daily compliance checks, workflow audits.
  • BDC Manager: Weekly audits, call grading, template grading.
  • BDC Director: Multi-store oversight, monthly audits.
  • GM/Owner: Approve compliance policies and corrections.
  • Executive Team: Oversee enterprise compliance strategy.
Compliance Cadence
  • Daily: Texting, template, workflow compliance checks.
  • Weekly: Manager compliance audit, call grading, CRM audit.
  • Monthly: Store scorecard, director review.
  • Quarterly: Executive audit, risk assessment.
  • Annual: Full enterprise audit, training refresh.
