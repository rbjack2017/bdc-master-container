  • Alternate channels for quiet leads
  • Use appointment protection checklists
  • Send response boosters to increase reply rate
Response Boosters 
Use these short phrases at the end of emails, texts, or voicemails:

• “Want me to send options?”
• “What’s most important to you?”
• “Do you want the best match or a few choices?”
• “Would you like me to include similar vehicles?”
• “Want me to check availability?”
• “Would a quick video help?”
Appointment Word Tracks 
• “I can confirm your appointment for {{Date}} at {{Time}}.”
• “Would you like a reminder text the day before?”
• “I’ll have the vehicle ready for you.”
• “Is there anything you want me to prepare before you arrive?”
Objection Word Tracks
  • Price: “I can show similar options or explain what affects pricing.”
  • Payment: “Would you consider different terms or similar models?”
  • Timing: “What timeline works best for you?”
  • Trade Value: “Could you send VIN, miles, and photos?”
  • Availability: “I can check incoming options or similar inventory.”
  • Just Looking: “What features matter most to you?”
