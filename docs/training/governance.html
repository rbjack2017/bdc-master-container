Governance Module

The Governance Module defines the rules, expectations, and operating standards for the BDC. It ensures consistency, fairness, compliance, and accountability across all communication and workflow processes.

1. Purpose of Governance

  • Protect dealership reputation
  • Ensure consistent customer experience
  • Maintain workflow accuracy
  • Support compliance and QA standards
  • Provide clear expectations for reps
  • Define consequences for rule violations

2. Core Governance Rules

Availability Rules

  • Reps must be fully available before responding to opportunities
  • No responding from home unless explicitly approved
  • No emojis in customer communication
  • No back-to-back opportunity claiming

Opportunity Rules

  • Respond within 10 minutes
  • Follow the 5-minute open-to-all rule
  • Follow the 3-op limit
  • Log all communication in CRM

Communication Rules

  • No pricing in text messages
  • No payment quotes
  • No guarantees (“approval,” “rate,” “value”)
  • Professional tone at all times

3. Workflow Governance

Reps must follow all workflow steps exactly as defined in the Workflows Module.

  • Day 1 email must include OEM-required elements
  • All tasks must be completed on time
  • Inactivation must occur on Day 7
  • No tasks after inactivation

4. Compliance Governance

Compliance rules must be followed at all times. Violations result in coaching or disciplinary action.

  • No misleading statements
  • No unverifiable claims
  • No conditional guarantees
  • No discussing credit details outside secure application

5. QA Governance

QA scores directly impact opportunity eligibility and coaching requirements.

  • QA score below 70 triggers coaching
  • QA score below 60 triggers restricted opportunity access
  • QA score below 50 triggers formal review

6. Rule Violation System

Violations are tracked in the Rule Violation Dashboard.

Minor Violations

  • Late responses
  • Missing CRM notes
  • Incorrect source logging

Major Violations

  • Pricing in text messages
  • Guarantees or misleading statements
  • Responding while unavailable
  • Ignoring workflow steps

7. Governance Summary

Governance ensures fairness, consistency, compliance, and professionalism across the entire BDC. It is the foundation of the BDC Operating System.
