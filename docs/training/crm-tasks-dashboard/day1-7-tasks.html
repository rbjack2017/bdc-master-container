Day 1–7 Task Structure

The complete 7‑day CRM task structure for new leads, ensuring continuity, accountability, and proper follow‑up timing.

Overview

This module outlines the full Day 1–7 CRM task structure: calls, texts, emails, manager touches, value‑add messages, and final close‑loop tasks. Every step is designed to maintain momentum and prevent lead loss.

Standards

  • Every action must generate a next task.
  • Use correct call types and sources.
  • Follow the exact Day 1–7 sequence without skipping steps.

Examples

Day 1: Two calls, text, personal email, afternoon email. Day 2: Call + manager email. Day 7: Final call + final email + inactivation.
