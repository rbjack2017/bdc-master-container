Task Timing Rules

Timing rules for calls, texts, emails, and follow‑ups to maintain momentum and prevent lead loss.

Overview

Task timing ensures leads move through the workflow at the correct pace. This module covers call spacing, daily follow‑up timing, and when to escalate or close out leads.

Standards

  • Day 1 requires multiple touches.
  • Day 2–7 require one strong action per day.
  • Never let a lead go more than 24 hours without a task.

Examples

“Day 1: Two calls before noon, one call afternoon, text + email.” “Day 7: Final call + final email + inactivation.”
