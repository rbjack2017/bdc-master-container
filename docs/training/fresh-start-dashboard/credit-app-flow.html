Credit Application Flow

How to guide Fresh Start customers through the online credit application and next steps.

Overview

This module explains how to walk customers through the credit application process, what information they need, and how to set expectations for lender review and next steps.

Standards

  • Explain the application in simple, clear steps.
  • Tell customers what documents they may need.
  • Clarify that multiple lenders may review their application.

Examples

“Once you complete the application, our Fresh Start lenders will review it and let us know what programs you qualify for. I’ll walk you through each step.”
