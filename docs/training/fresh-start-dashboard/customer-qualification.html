Customer Qualification

How to determine whether a customer qualifies for Fresh Start programs and what information is needed.

Overview

Qualification depends on income, employment stability, down payment availability, credit history, and lender program requirements. This module teaches how to gather the right information quickly.

Standards

  • Ask about income and employment early.
  • Clarify down payment expectations.
  • Explain how lenders evaluate credit challenges.

Examples

“Most Fresh Start lenders look for stable income and a small down payment. I can help you figure out which programs you qualify for.”
