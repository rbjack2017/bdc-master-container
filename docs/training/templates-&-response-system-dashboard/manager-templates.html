Manager Templates

Templates for manager‑level follow‑ups, save‑a‑deal messages, and appointment confirmations.

Overview

This module contains communication templates used when a manager steps in: escalation, save‑a‑deal, special approvals, and high‑value appointments.

Standards

  • Reassure the customer and reduce friction.
  • Clarify next steps and timing.
  • Support the rep’s messaging, not contradict it.

Example Templates

Manager Save‑a‑Deal Email:

“Hi {{First Name}}, I’m {{Manager Name}}, one of the managers here at {{Store}}. {{Rep Name}} shared your situation with me, and I’d like to help make this easy. What’s the most important thing we need to get right for you?”
