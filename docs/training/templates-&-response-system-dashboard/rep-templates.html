Rep Templates

Core email, text, and voicemail templates for BDC reps, built on one‑question messaging.

Overview

This module contains the everyday templates reps use for new leads, price requests, trade‑ins, quiet leads, redirects, and appointment confirmations.

Standards

  • One main question per message.
  • Short, specific, and easy to answer.
  • All placeholders must be replaced before sending.

Example Templates

New Lead Text:

“Hi {{First Name}}, this is {{Your Name}} with {{Store}}. I got your request on the {{Vehicle}} — are you comparing a few options or focusing on this one?”

Quiet Lead Text:

“Still here to help with the {{Vehicle}}. What’s the best next step for you — options, numbers, or a quick visit?”
