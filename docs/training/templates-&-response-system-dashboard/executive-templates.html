Executive Templates

High‑level communication from GM, Director, or Owner for recovery, apology, and special situations.

Overview

This module contains templates for executive‑level outreach: apologies, recovery offers, and high‑trust messages when things go wrong.

Standards

  • Take ownership and avoid blame.
  • Offer a clear path to resolution.
  • Reinforce trust and long‑term relationship.

Example Templates

Executive Apology Email:

“Hi {{First Name}}, I’m {{Exec Name}}, {{Title}} at {{Store}}. I’m sorry for the frustration you’ve experienced — that’s not the standard we aim for. I’d like to personally help make this right. What would feel like a fair next step from your perspective?”
