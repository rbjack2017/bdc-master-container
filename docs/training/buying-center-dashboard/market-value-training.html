Market Value Training

How market demand, reconditioning, and inventory needs affect appraisal values.

Overview

Market value is influenced by demand, seasonality, auction trends, reconditioning costs, and how quickly the dealership can retail the vehicle.

Standards

  • Explain demand and seasonality.
  • Clarify reconditioning impact.
  • Use market trends to support value conversations.

Examples

“Vehicles with lower reconditioning costs often receive stronger offers because they can be retailed faster.”
