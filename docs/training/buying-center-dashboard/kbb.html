KBB Value Training

Understanding Kelley Blue Book values and how they relate to real appraisal numbers.

Overview

KBB values are estimates based on market averages. Real appraisal values depend on condition, equipment, mileage, demand, and reconditioning costs.

Standards

  • Explain KBB as a starting point, not a final number.
  • Clarify that in‑person appraisal gives the strongest value.
  • Use KBB ranges to set expectations, not promises.

Examples

“KBB is a great starting point, but our appraisers can often offer more or less depending on condition and market demand.”
