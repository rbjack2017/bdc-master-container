Buying Center Scripts

All Buying Center text and email scripts used for sell/trade conversations.

Overview

Includes Buying Center texts, Buying Center emails, sell vs trade scripts, value conversations, and appraisal scheduling messages.

Standards

  • Short, clear, and easy to answer.
  • One question per message.
  • Always offer next step options.

Examples

“We buy vehicles every day and make the process simple, quick, and hassle‑free. Are you leaning toward selling or trading?”
