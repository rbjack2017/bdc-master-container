Sell vs Trade Intent

How to identify whether the customer wants to sell outright or trade toward another vehicle.

Overview

This module covers the Buying Center’s first step: determining the customer’s intent. Your messaging sets the tone for the entire process and helps guide the next steps.

Standards

  • Ask directly: “Are you leaning toward selling or trading?”
  • Explain both options clearly and simply.
  • Keep the process feeling quick, easy, and transparent.

Examples

Buying Center Text: “We buy vehicles every day and make the process simple, quick, and hassle‑free. Which are you leaning toward — selling or trading?”
