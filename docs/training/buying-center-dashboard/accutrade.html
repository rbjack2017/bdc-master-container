AccuTrade Value Training

How AccuTrade evaluates vehicles and why it provides more precise values than generic tools.

Overview

AccuTrade uses VIN‑specific data, condition scoring, equipment verification, and real‑time market demand to produce accurate appraisal values.

Standards

  • Explain AccuTrade as VIN‑specific and condition‑based.
  • Use AccuTrade results to support transparent value conversations.
  • Clarify that final numbers require in‑person inspection.

Examples

“AccuTrade gives us a VIN‑specific value based on your exact equipment and condition, not just averages.”
