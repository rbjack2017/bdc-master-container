Appraisal Preparation

How to prepare customers and vehicles for the strongest appraisal value.

Overview

Covers what customers should bring, how to explain the appraisal process, and how to set expectations for timing and value.

Standards

  • Explain what appraisers look for.
  • Clarify that clean vehicles often appraise higher.
  • Ensure customers bring payoff info if financed.

Examples

“The best way to get the strongest value is a quick in‑person appraisal. Would today or tomorrow work better?”
