Redirects Module

The Redirects Module teaches reps how to professionally redirect customers when their vehicle of interest is sold, unavailable, delayed, or not a match. Redirects protect the customer experience and keep the conversation alive.

1. Purpose of Redirects

  • Prevent dead ends when a vehicle sells
  • Offer similar or better options
  • Maintain customer engagement
  • Protect workflow continuity
  • Support sales team inventory strategy

2. Redirect Rules

Core Rules

  • Always acknowledge the customer’s interest
  • Never say “It’s gone” without offering alternatives
  • Always offer similar options
  • Always ask one low‑effort question
  • Always update CRM with correct status

Compliance Rules

  • No pricing in text messages
  • No guarantees
  • No misleading statements
  • No pressure language

3. Vehicle Sold Redirect Templates

Sold Redirect Text

Hi {{First Name}}, this is Raven with Bachman. The {{Vehicle of Interest}} has sold, but I can help find similar options if you’d like. What matters most — price, mileage, color, or features?

Sold Redirect Email

Subject: Update on the {{Vehicle of Interest}}

Hi {{First Name}}, I wanted to update you — the {{Vehicle of Interest}} has sold. If you’re still searching, I can help with similar options, incoming units, or vehicles with the same features. What matters most to you?

4. Similar Options Redirect

Text Option

Hi {{First Name}}, I have a few similar options to the {{Vehicle of Interest}}. Would you prefer closest match, lowest mileage, or best price?

Email Option

Subject: Similar Options to the {{Vehicle of Interest}}

Hi {{First Name}}, I pulled a few similar options that match what you were looking for. If you want, I can send closest match, lowest mileage, or best price first. Which works best?

5. Incoming Unit Redirect

Text Option

Hi {{First Name}}, we have similar units arriving soon. Would you prefer incoming options or available ones?

Email Option

Subject: Incoming Options You May Like

Hi {{First Name}}, we have incoming units similar to the {{Vehicle of Interest}}. If you’d like, I can send details on incoming options or available ones. Which works best?

6. Trim/Feature Redirect

Text Option

Hi {{First Name}}, if features matter most, I can send options with the same package or similar equipment. What’s most important — safety features, tech, or comfort?

Email Option

Subject: Options with Similar Features

Hi {{First Name}}, I can send options with the same features as the {{Vehicle of Interest}} — safety, tech, or comfort packages. Which matters most?

7. Redirect Workflow

Step 1 — Identify Redirect Trigger

  • Vehicle sold
  • Vehicle unavailable
  • Vehicle delayed
  • Customer’s needs changed

Step 2 — Send Redirect Message

  • Sold Redirect Text
  • Sold Redirect Email
  • Similar Options Redirect
  • Incoming Unit Redirect

Step 3 — Offer Options

  • Closest match
  • Lowest mileage
  • Best price
  • Incoming units
  • Feature‑matched units

Step 4 — Update CRM

  • Correct status
  • Correct notes
  • Correct next task

8. Redirect Customization Rules

Before sending any redirect message:

  • Replace placeholders
  • Remove anything that doesn’t apply
  • Ask one main question
  • Match customer tone
  • End with one clear next step

Source: Quick Message Customization Tips

9. Redirect Violations

Minor Violations

  • Not offering alternatives
  • Missing placeholders
  • Multiple questions

Major Violations

  • Telling customer “It’s gone” with no options
  • Pricing in text messages
  • Guarantees
  • Misleading statements

10. Redirect Summary

Redirects keep customers engaged even when their original vehicle is unavailable. Offering similar options, incoming units, or feature‑matched vehicles ensures a smooth, professional experience.
