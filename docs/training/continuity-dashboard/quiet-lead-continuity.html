Quiet Lead Continuity

How to maintain contact with customers who stop responding without overwhelming them.

Overview

Quiet leads require gentle, low-pressure follow-ups. This module teaches how to re-engage customers using simple, one-question messages that respect their time and keep the door open.

Standards

  • Use soft, low-pressure messaging.
  • Ask one simple question.
  • Offer options without assuming urgency.

Examples

“Still here to help with the {{Vehicle}} — what’s the best next step?” “Would you prefer options, numbers, or a quick visit?”
