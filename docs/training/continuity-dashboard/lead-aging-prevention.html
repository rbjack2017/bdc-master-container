Lead Aging Prevention

How to prevent leads from aging past 24–48 hours without contact or next steps.

Overview

Lead aging happens when customers go too long without communication. This module teaches how to maintain momentum, keep leads active, and avoid losing opportunities due to inactivity.

Standards

  • No lead should go more than 24 hours without contact.
  • Every lead must have a scheduled next step.
  • Use quiet lead messaging when customers stop responding.

Examples

“Still here to help — what’s the best next step for you?” “Would you like me to keep this open, or follow up later?”
