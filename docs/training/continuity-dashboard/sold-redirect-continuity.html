Sold Redirect Continuity

How to maintain continuity when a customer’s vehicle of interest has sold.

Overview

Sold Redirect Continuity ensures customers stay engaged even after their vehicle sells. This module teaches how to redirect interest, discover must-haves, and offer strong alternatives.

Standards

  • Acknowledge sold status clearly.
  • Ask what mattered most about the original vehicle.
  • Offer 2–3 strong alternatives.

Examples

“The {{Vehicle}} has sold, but I can help find similar options. What mattered most — price, features, mileage, or color?”
