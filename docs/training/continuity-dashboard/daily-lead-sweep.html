Daily Lead Sweep

The daily process for reviewing, updating, and advancing all active leads to prevent stagnation.

Overview

Daily Lead Sweep ensures every active lead receives attention. This includes checking new leads, updating tasks, sending follow-ups, and ensuring no customer goes more than 24 hours without contact.

Standards

  • Review all leads at least once per day.
  • Ensure every lead has a next step scheduled.
  • Send follow-ups using one-question messaging.

Examples

“Just checking in — what’s the best next step for you?” “Are you still considering this vehicle, or should I circle back later?”
