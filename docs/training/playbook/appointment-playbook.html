Appointment Playbook

Word tracks, emails, texts, and workflows for setting, confirming, and supporting priority appointments.

Overview

This module covers appointment word tracks, follow‑up messages, confirmation emails and texts, and how to explain priority appointments so customers understand the value and feel confident about visiting.

Standards

  • Emphasize transparency and efficiency in the visit.
  • Explain that vehicles are pulled up and ready, and trades can be appraised during test drives.
  • Always confirm date, time, store, and what to bring (license, insurance).
