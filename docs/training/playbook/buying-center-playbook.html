Buying Center Playbook

Scripts and workflows for the Authorized Buying Center, focused on customers selling or trading vehicles.

Overview

This module covers Buying Center texts and emails, intent discovery (sell vs trade), value conversations, and how to make the process feel simple, quick, and transparent for customers.

Standards

  • Clarify whether the customer is leaning toward selling or trading.
  • Explain that you buy vehicles every day and streamline the process.
  • Offer both online start links and in‑person appraisal options.
