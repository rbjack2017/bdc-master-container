Email Playbook

Core email sequences and templates for new leads, price requests, trade‑ins, quiet leads, sold redirects, and appointments.

Overview

This module covers the full email playbook: Day 1–7 sequences, price request emails, trade‑in emails, quiet lead follow‑ups, sold redirect messages, and appointment confirmations. The goal is consistent, clear, and easy‑to‑answer communication.

Standards

  • Use one main question per email.
  • Replace all placeholders before sending.
  • Match tone and keep messages short and specific.
