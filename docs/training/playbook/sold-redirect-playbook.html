Sold Redirect Playbook

Templates and workflows for when a vehicle has sold, focusing on redirecting customers to similar options that still fit their needs.

Overview

This module covers Day 1–7 sold redirect emails and texts, plus strategies for discovering must‑haves, aligning budget, and inviting customers to compare alternatives in person.

Standards

  • Acknowledge the sold status clearly and empathetically.
  • Ask what mattered most about the original vehicle.
  • Offer 2–3 strong alternatives instead of generic lists.
