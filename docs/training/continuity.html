Continuity Module

The Continuity Module ensures that customer follow-up continues smoothly even when reps are unavailable. It prevents workflow gaps, protects the customer experience, and maintains consistent communication across the BDC.

1. Purpose of Continuity

  • Ensure customers never go without follow-up
  • Maintain workflow accuracy during absences
  • Protect dealership reputation
  • Support team-based coverage
  • Prevent missed opportunities
  • Provide clear rules for handoffs and coverage

2. Continuity Coverage Rules

When a Rep Is Unavailable

  • All active leads must be reassigned
  • All tasks must be redistributed
  • All appointments must be reassigned
  • All new opportunities must be blocked

Approved Reasons for Coverage

  • Scheduled PTO
  • Illness
  • Emergency
  • Training
  • System issues

Unapproved Reasons

  • Slow day
  • Personal errands
  • “I didn’t feel like it”
  • Responding from home without approval

3. Continuity Workflow

Continuity follows a structured process to ensure seamless coverage.

Step 1 — Identify Absence

  • Rep notifies leadership
  • Leadership confirms coverage need

Step 2 — Reassign Active Leads

  • All active leads moved to available reps
  • All tasks reassigned
  • All appointments reassigned

Step 3 — Update Continuity Dashboard

  • Record rep absence
  • Record coverage assignments
  • Record workflow status

Step 4 — Resume Normal Workflow

  • Rep returns
  • Leads may be reassigned back if appropriate
  • Dashboard updated

4. Continuity Communication Standards

Coverage communication must be clear, professional, and consistent.

Internal Communication

  • Notify team of coverage assignments
  • Confirm who owns each lead
  • Confirm who owns each appointment

Customer Communication

  • Introduce yourself as the new point of contact
  • Explain that you’re assisting the customer
  • Maintain workflow timing

5. Continuity Compliance

Continuity must follow compliance rules at all times.

  • No responding while unavailable
  • No responding from home without approval
  • No skipping workflow steps
  • No ignoring tasks during coverage

6. Continuity Dashboard

The Continuity Dashboard tracks all coverage activity.

Dashboard Tracks:

  • Rep absences
  • Coverage assignments
  • Lead reassignments
  • Task redistribution
  • Appointment coverage
  • Workflow status

7. Continuity Violations

Violations occur when continuity rules are not followed.

Minor Violations

  • Late coverage communication
  • Missing coverage notes
  • Incorrect task reassignment

Major Violations

  • Responding while unavailable
  • Ignoring coverage assignments
  • Skipping workflow steps during coverage
  • Failing to reassign leads

8. Continuity Summary

Continuity ensures seamless customer follow-up, protects workflow integrity, and maintains professional communication even during rep absences. It is essential for consistent BDC performance.
