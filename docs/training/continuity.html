Continuity Module

The Continuity Module ensures that customer opportunities are handled smoothly, even when reps are unavailable. It prevents gaps, delays, and missed communication by defining clear coverage rules and handoff procedures.

1. Coverage Rules

  • Reps must notify leadership before stepping away
  • Coverage must be assigned for any break longer than 5 minutes
  • Coverage reps must follow all workflow rules
  • Coverage ends when the original rep returns

2. Handoff Structure

  • All handoffs must be documented in CRM
  • Coverage rep must leave a note explaining actions taken
  • Original rep must review coverage notes upon return
  • Customer communication must remain seamless

3. Workflow Stability

  • Tasks must never be skipped due to rep absence
  • Coverage reps must complete all assigned tasks
  • Opportunities must not be left idle
  • All communication must remain compliant

4. Continuity Summary

Continuity ensures that customers receive consistent communication and workflow accuracy, regardless of rep availability.
