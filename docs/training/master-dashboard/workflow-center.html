Workflow Center

The unified workflow system for new leads, price requests, trade/sell, quiet leads, appointments, sold redirects, and buying center.

Overview

The Workflow Center contains every operational workflow used in the BDC OS. Each workflow is standardized, documented, and tied directly to CRM tasks and playbooks.

Standards

  • Every workflow follows the Day 1–7 structure.
  • Every workflow integrates with CRM tasks.
  • Every workflow uses one-question messaging.
