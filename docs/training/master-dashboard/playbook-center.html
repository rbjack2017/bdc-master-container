Playbook Center

Central access point for all messaging playbooks: email, text, voicemail, sold redirect, trade/sell, buying center, fresh start, and appointments.

Overview

The Playbook Center houses every messaging template and sequence used in the BDC OS. This module explains how playbooks are structured and how they integrate with workflows.

Standards

  • One-question messaging across all templates.
  • Short, specific, easy-to-answer communication.
  • Every template includes a clear next step.
