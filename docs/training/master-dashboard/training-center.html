Training Center

The central hub for all BDC training modules: customization rules, objections, appointments, buying center, sold redirect, and more.

Overview

The Training Center contains every skill module used to develop BDC mastery. This includes messaging customization, objection handling, appointment training, buying center training, and sold redirect training.

Standards

  • Training modules follow the same structure as dashboards.
  • Every module includes standards and examples.
  • Completion is tracked through the QA system.
