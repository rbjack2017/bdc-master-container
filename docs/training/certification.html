Certification Pathway (BDCCR)

The BDCCR Certification Pathway defines the training levels, requirements, and advancement structure for all BDC representatives. Certification ensures mastery of communication, workflows, compliance, and performance standards.

1. Purpose of Certification

  • Ensure reps understand all BDC systems
  • Provide structured skill development
  • Protect workflow accuracy
  • Maintain compliance
  • Support career growth
  • Reward high performance

2. Certification Levels

The BDCCR pathway includes four levels:

Level 1 — Foundation

  • Basic communication skills
  • Understanding workflows
  • Basic CRM navigation
  • Compliance fundamentals
  • Templates usage

Level 2 — Core

  • Full workflow mastery
  • Accurate CRM documentation
  • Objection handling
  • Task discipline
  • Appointment setting

Level 3 — Advanced

  • High-level objection handling
  • Redirect mastery
  • Continuity coverage
  • High compliance accuracy
  • Advanced communication

Level 4 — Expert

  • Leadership readiness
  • Coaching ability
  • Workflow auditing
  • High KPI performance
  • Consistency across all modules

3. Certification Requirements

Level 1 Requirements

  • Complete Foundation Training
  • Pass Foundation Quiz (80%+)
  • Demonstrate template accuracy
  • Complete onboarding tasks

Level 2 Requirements

  • Pass Core Training (85%+)
  • Maintain workflow accuracy (100%)
  • Maintain compliance score (95%+)
  • Demonstrate correct objection handling
  • Pass call review

Level 3 Requirements

  • Pass Advanced Training (90%+)
  • Maintain compliance score (100%)
  • Maintain KPI benchmarks
  • Demonstrate redirect mastery
  • Pass advanced call review

Level 4 Requirements

  • Pass Expert Training (95%+)
  • Maintain top-tier KPIs
  • Demonstrate coaching ability
  • Pass leadership readiness review
  • Complete workflow audit

4. Certification Timeline

Certification follows a structured timeline to ensure mastery at each level.

Level 1 Timeline

  • Week 1–2: Foundation Training
  • Week 3: Foundation Quiz
  • Week 4: Onboarding completion

Level 2 Timeline

  • Month 2–3: Core Training
  • Month 3: Core Quiz
  • Month 3: Call review

Level 3 Timeline

  • Month 4–6: Advanced Training
  • Month 6: Advanced Quiz
  • Month 6: Redirect mastery review

Level 4 Timeline

  • Month 7–12: Expert Training
  • Month 12: Leadership readiness review
  • Month 12: Workflow audit

5. Certification Assessments

Each level includes assessments to ensure mastery.

Assessment Types

  • Quizzes
  • Call reviews
  • Email reviews
  • Text reviews
  • Workflow audits
  • Compliance audits

6. Certification Dashboard

The Certification Dashboard tracks rep progress across all levels.

Dashboard Tracks:

  • Training completion
  • Quiz scores
  • Compliance scores
  • Workflow accuracy
  • KPI benchmarks
  • Call review results
  • Level advancement

7. Certification Violations

Violations occur when reps fail to meet certification standards.

Minor Violations

  • Late training completion
  • Missed quizzes
  • Incomplete modules

Major Violations

  • Compliance failures
  • Workflow failures
  • Repeated KPI underperformance
  • Refusal to complete training

8. Certification Summary

The BDCCR Certification Pathway ensures reps develop the skills, discipline, and consistency required for high-level BDC performance. Certification supports career growth, protects workflow accuracy, and maintains compliance.
