Buying Center Module

The Buying Center Module defines how to handle leads who want to sell or trade their vehicle. This includes Day 1–3 communication, appraisal information gathering, low‑effort reply prompts, and compliant messaging.

1. Purpose of the Buying Center

  • Acquire inventory through customer vehicles
  • Provide fast, simple appraisal communication
  • Support trade conversations for sales opportunities
  • Deliver consistent, compliant messaging
  • Gather accurate vehicle details before handoff

2. Buying Center Lead Types

  • Sell‑only customers
  • Trade‑in customers
  • Appraisal‑only customers
  • Fresh Start / credit challenge customers with trades

5. Required Vehicle Details

Once the customer engages, gather the following:

  • Year
  • Make
  • Model
  • Mileage
  • VIN
  • Payoff status
  • Vehicle condition
  • Photos (optional but helpful)
  • Preferred appraisal timing

Source: Buying Center Internal Notes

6. Buying Center Workflow

Step 1 — Identify Lead Type

  • Sell
  • Trade
  • Appraisal

Step 2 — Send Day 1 Message

  • Day 1 Text
  • Day 1 Email

Step 3 — Gather Vehicle Details

  • Mileage
  • VIN
  • Payoff
  • Condition

Step 4 — Schedule Appraisal or Handoff

  • In‑store appraisal
  • Phone review
  • Later follow‑up

Step 5 — Day 2 & Day 3 Follow‑Up

  • Day 2 Follow‑Up Email
  • Day 3 Final Check‑In

7. Buying Center Compliance

  • No guaranteed values
  • No pricing in text messages
  • No appraisal ranges without manager approval
  • No pressure language

8. Buying Center Summary

The Buying Center workflow ensures consistent communication, accurate appraisal information, and simple next steps for customers looking to sell or trade their vehicle. Day 1–3 templates, low‑effort replies, and correct detail gathering create a smooth and professional experience.
