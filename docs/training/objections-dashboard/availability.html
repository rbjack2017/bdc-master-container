Availability Objections

How to handle concerns about whether a vehicle is still available or when new inventory will arrive.

Overview

Availability objections occur when customers worry about missing out or when a vehicle has sold. This module teaches how to reassure customers, provide options, and guide them toward next steps.

Standards

  • Confirm availability quickly and clearly.
  • If sold, redirect with strong alternatives.
  • Offer to notify them when new inventory arrives.

Examples

“That one is still available — would you like to stop in today or tomorrow?” “It has sold, but I can help find similar options. What mattered most about it?”
