Trade Objections

How to handle trade‑in value concerns and explain appraisal logic clearly.

Overview

Trade objections usually come from online estimates, emotional value, or misunderstanding of appraisal factors. This module teaches how to explain value clearly and guide customers toward an in‑person appraisal.

Standards

  • Acknowledge online estimates without dismissing them.
  • Explain condition, equipment, mileage, and demand.
  • Invite them for a quick in‑person appraisal.

Examples

“Online tools are helpful, but they can’t see condition or equipment. A quick in‑person appraisal gives you the strongest and most accurate number. Would today or tomorrow work better?”
