Credit Objections

How to handle credit concerns with empathy and clear next steps.

Overview

Credit objections often come from fear, embarrassment, or past experiences. This module teaches how to reassure customers and guide them toward Fresh Start options.

Standards

  • Use empathetic, non‑judgmental language.
  • Explain lender variety and Fresh Start programs.
  • Guide them to the online application confidently.

Examples

“A lot of people are rebuilding credit right now — you’re not alone. We work with lenders who specialize in Fresh Start programs. I can walk you through the next step whenever you're ready.”
