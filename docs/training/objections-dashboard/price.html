Price Objections

How to handle price concerns with transparency, clarity, and confidence.

Overview

Price objections are the most common customer concern. This module teaches how to respond with clarity, explain market value, and guide customers toward next steps without pressure.

Standards

  • Acknowledge the concern without arguing.
  • Explain how pricing is set (market, availability, equipment).
  • Redirect to next step (visit, appraisal, options).

Examples

“I completely understand — price is important. The reason this one is priced where it is comes down to condition and current market demand. Would you like to see similar options in different price ranges?”
