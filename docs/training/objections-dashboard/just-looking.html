“Just Looking” Objections

How to handle customers who are browsing, undecided, or not ready to engage deeply.

Overview

“Just looking” is not a dead lead — it’s an early‑stage lead. This module teaches how to ask simple questions that move the conversation forward without pressure.

Standards

  • Use soft, exploratory questions.
  • Offer help without assuming urgency.
  • Keep communication light and easy.

Examples

“Totally fine — what caught your eye about this one?” “Are you comparing a few options or just browsing right now?”
