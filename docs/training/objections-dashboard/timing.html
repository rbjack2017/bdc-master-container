Timing Objections

How to handle “not ready yet,” “maybe later,” and “just checking” timing concerns.

Overview

Timing objections are not rejections — they are opportunities to understand the customer’s timeline and adjust follow‑up accordingly. This module teaches how to keep leads warm without pressure.

Standards

  • Ask when they want you to follow up.
  • Offer options (later, next week, next month).
  • Keep the door open with soft messaging.

Examples

“Totally fine — what timing works best for you? I can follow up later, or keep this open if you want to stay updated.”
