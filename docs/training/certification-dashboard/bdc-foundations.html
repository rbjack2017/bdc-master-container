BDC Foundations

Core fundamentals every BDC professional must master: communication, lead handling, tone, and daily workflow.

Overview

This module covers the essential building blocks of BDC performance: lead response structure, tone control, one‑question messaging, and daily continuity habits that prevent lead aging.

Standards

  • Respond quickly with clear next steps.
  • Use one main question per message.
  • Match tone and keep communication simple.
