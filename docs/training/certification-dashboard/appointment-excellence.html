Appointment Excellence

Mastery of appointment setting, confirmation, and visit preparation.

Overview

This module covers appointment word tracks, confirmation messaging, visit preparation, and how to explain priority appointments to increase show rates.

Standards

  • Always confirm date, time, store, and what to bring.
  • Explain that vehicles are pulled up and ready.
  • Offer appraisal during the test drive when applicable.
