Voicemail Playbook

Core voicemail scripts for new leads, price requests, trade‑ins, quiet leads, and appointment confirmations.

Overview

This module outlines voicemail templates designed to be short, clear, and paired with follow‑up texts. Each script sets expectations and directs the customer to the next step.

Standards

  • Keep voicemails under 30–40 seconds.
  • Mention that a text will follow with your direct line.
  • State who you are, why you’re calling, and the next step.
