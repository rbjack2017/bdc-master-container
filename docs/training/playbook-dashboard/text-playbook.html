Text Playbook

Core text templates and Day 1–7 sequences aligned with email focus: discovery, fit, budget, trade, appointment, contact preference, and decision.

Overview

This module covers all text templates, including new lead texts, price texts, trade texts, quiet lead texts, appointment texts, and the Day 1–7 text sequence that mirrors the email focus for each day.

Standards

  • Keep texts short and easy to answer.
  • Ask one clear question per text.
  • Avoid long paragraphs; focus on next step.
