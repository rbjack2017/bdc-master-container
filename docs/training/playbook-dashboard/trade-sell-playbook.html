Trade/Sell Playbook

Day 1–7 sequences and templates for customers looking to sell outright or trade toward another vehicle.

Overview

This module includes the Trade/Sell Day 1–7 email and text sequences, plus scripts for gathering vehicle details, condition, payoff information, and scheduling in‑person appraisals.

Standards

  • Clarify whether the customer is selling, trading, or just gathering information.
  • Collect year, make, model, mileage, VIN, and photos when possible.
  • Explain payoff vs title clearly and simply.

Examples

Refer to Trade‑In Email, Trade‑In Text, Buying Center texts, and the Trade/Sell Day 1–7 sequence in the trade/sell library.
