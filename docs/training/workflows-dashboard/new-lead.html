New Lead Workflow

The full Day 1–7 workflow for new leads: calls, texts, emails, tasks, and continuity.

Overview

The New Lead Workflow is the foundation of the BDC OS. It outlines every action from the moment a lead arrives through Day 7 close‑out.

Standards

  • Day 1 requires multiple touches.
  • Every action must generate a next task.
  • Follow the exact Day 1–7 structure without skipping steps.

Workflow Steps

Day 1: Two calls before noon, text, personal email, afternoon call, afternoon email. Day 2–6: One strong action per day. Day 7: Final call + final email + inactivation.

Examples

“Just checking in — what’s the best next step for you?” “Are you comparing a few options or focusing on this one?”
