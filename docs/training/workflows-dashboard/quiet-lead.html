Quiet Lead Workflow

How to re‑engage customers who stop responding using soft, low‑pressure messaging.

Overview

Quiet leads require gentle follow‑ups. This workflow teaches how to keep the door open without overwhelming the customer.

Standards

  • Use soft, low‑pressure messaging.
  • Ask one simple question.
  • Offer options without assuming urgency.

Workflow Steps

Step 1: Soft check‑in. Step 2: Ask one simple question. Step 3: Offer options (later, next week, alternatives). Step 4: Set next task based on their timing.

Examples

“Still here to help — what’s the best next step for you?” “Would you prefer options, numbers, or a quick visit?”
