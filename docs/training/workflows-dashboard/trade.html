Trade Workflow

How to handle trade‑in leads, online estimates, appraisal scheduling, and value concerns.

Overview

The Trade Workflow teaches how to respond to trade‑in inquiries, explain appraisal logic, and guide customers toward an in‑person appraisal for the strongest number.

Standards

  • Acknowledge online estimates without dismissing them.
  • Explain condition, equipment, mileage, and demand.
  • Invite them for a quick appraisal.

Workflow Steps

Step 1: Acknowledge their estimate. Step 2: Explain appraisal factors. Step 3: Offer a quick in‑person appraisal. Step 4: Schedule visit or redirect to alternatives.

Examples

“Online tools are helpful, but they can’t see condition or equipment. Would today or tomorrow work better for a quick appraisal?”
