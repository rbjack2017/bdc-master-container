Sold Redirect Workflow

How to redirect customers when their vehicle has sold using must‑have discovery and strong alternatives.

Overview

Sold Redirect is used when a customer’s vehicle of interest is no longer available. This workflow teaches how to keep the customer engaged using must‑have discovery and alternative options.

Standards

  • Acknowledge sold status clearly.
  • Ask what mattered most.
  • Offer 2–3 strong alternatives.

Workflow Steps

Step 1: Acknowledge sold status. Step 2: Ask must‑have discovery question. Step 3: Present alternatives. Step 4: Ask which fits best.

Examples

“It has sold, but I can help find similar options. What mattered most — price, features, mileage, or color?”
