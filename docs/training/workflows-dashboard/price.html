Price Workflow

How to handle price requests, price objections, and price‑driven leads using one‑question messaging.

Overview

The Price Workflow teaches how to respond to price requests quickly, clearly, and confidently, while guiding customers toward next steps like visits or alternative options.

Standards

  • Acknowledge the price request immediately.
  • Provide clear pricing or explain availability.
  • Ask one simple next‑step question.

Workflow Steps

Step 1: Acknowledge the request. Step 2: Provide pricing or explain market value. Step 3: Ask a one‑question next step. Step 4: Schedule a visit or redirect to alternatives.

Examples

“I can help — what’s the best next step for you?” “Would you like to stop in today or tomorrow?”
