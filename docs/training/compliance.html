Compliance Module

The Compliance Module defines the rules that protect the dealership, the customer, and the BDC. Compliance ensures accuracy, honesty, professionalism, and legal protection in every communication channel.

1. Purpose of Compliance

  • Protect dealership reputation
  • Ensure legal and ethical communication
  • Prevent misinformation
  • Maintain OEM standards
  • Protect customers from confusion or pressure
  • Ensure consistent communication across the BDC

2. Core Compliance Rules

These rules apply to ALL communication — calls, emails, texts, and CRM notes.

Prohibited Statements

  • No pricing in text messages
  • No payment quotes
  • No guarantees (“approval,” “rate,” “value,” “you’ll get”)
  • No conditional promises (“as long as…”)
  • No discussing credit details outside secure application
  • No misleading statements
  • No pressure language

Required Standards

  • Professional tone at all times
  • Clear and accurate information
  • One main question per text
  • OEM-compliant Day 1 email
  • Accurate CRM documentation
  • Correct workflow steps

3. Compliance in Text Messages

Text messages must be short, compliant, and easy to answer.

Allowed in Texts

  • Availability questions
  • Low-effort questions
  • Appointment confirmations
  • Trade information requests
  • Redirect messages

Not Allowed in Texts

  • Pricing
  • Payments
  • Rates
  • Approval guarantees
  • VIN-specific quotes
  • Emojis

4. Compliance in Emails

Emails allow more detail but must still follow strict rules.

Allowed in Emails

  • Pricing (when accurate and verified)
  • Payment estimates (with disclaimers)
  • Trade value requests
  • OEM-compliant templates
  • Appointment confirmations

Not Allowed in Emails

  • Guarantees
  • Misleading statements
  • Unverified pricing
  • Unverified payment quotes

5. Compliance in Calls

Calls must be professional, accurate, and pressure-free.

Call Requirements

  • Warm, professional tone
  • Clear purpose
  • No pressure
  • No guarantees
  • No misleading statements
  • Accurate information only

Call Prohibitions

  • No “you’ll be approved”
  • No “you’ll get this rate”
  • No “your trade is worth X”
  • No “this price is guaranteed”

6. Compliance in CRM Notes

CRM notes must be accurate, professional, and compliant.

Required Note Elements

  • Purpose of interaction
  • Outcome of interaction
  • Customer response
  • Next step
  • Task created

Prohibited Notes

  • “Called”
  • “Left VM”
  • “Follow-up”
  • “Sent email”
  • Any pricing or payment quotes

7. Compliance Violations

Violations occur when reps break compliance rules. These are tracked in the QA and Rule Violation Dashboards.

Minor Violations

  • Missing placeholders
  • Overly long messages
  • Multiple questions in a text
  • Incorrect tone

Major Violations

  • Pricing in text messages
  • Payment quotes in text messages
  • Guarantees
  • Misleading statements
  • Unapproved templates
  • Incorrect credit discussions

8. Compliance Summary

Compliance protects the dealership, the customer, and the BDC. It ensures accuracy, professionalism, and trust. Every rep must follow compliance rules at all times to maintain a consistent and legally safe customer experience.
