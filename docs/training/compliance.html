Compliance Module

The Compliance Module defines the legal, ethical, and dealership‑required communication standards that all BDC reps must follow. Compliance protects the dealership, the customer, and the rep.

1. Core Compliance Rules

  • No pricing in text messages
  • No payment quotes
  • No guarantees (“approval,” “rate,” “value”)
  • No unverifiable claims
  • No misleading statements
  • No discussing credit details outside the secure application

2. Legal Sensitivity Areas

These topics require strict compliance and must be handled carefully.

  • Credit and financing
  • Interest rates
  • Trade‑in values
  • Approval status
  • Warranty coverage

3. Communication Restrictions

Reps must avoid language that implies certainty, guarantees, or promises.

  • Never say “You’re approved.”
  • Never say “Your payment will be…”
  • Never say “Your trade is worth…”
  • Never say “You’ll definitely get…”

4. Compliance in Text Messages

Text messages must be short, compliant, and free of restricted topics.

  • No pricing
  • No payments
  • No guarantees
  • No emojis
  • No slang

5. Compliance in Emails

Emails allow more detail but must still follow strict rules.

  • Use approved templates
  • Avoid conditional guarantees
  • Keep tone professional
  • Document all communication in CRM

6. Compliance Violations

Violations are tracked and reviewed by leadership.

Minor Violations

  • Incorrect source logging
  • Missing CRM notes
  • Late responses

Major Violations

  • Pricing in text messages
  • Guarantees or misleading statements
  • Discussing credit details improperly
  • Ignoring workflow steps

7. Compliance Summary

Compliance ensures legal protection, customer trust, and dealership integrity. It is one of the most important responsibilities of every BDC rep.
