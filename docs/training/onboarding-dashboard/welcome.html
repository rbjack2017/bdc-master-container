Welcome & Orientation

Introduction to the BDC OS, team expectations, culture, and your role in the customer experience.

Overview

This module introduces new team members to the BDC OS, explains how the system works, and outlines expectations for communication, professionalism, and daily workflow.

Standards

  • Follow the BDC OS structure for all communication.
  • Use one‑question messaging in all templates.
  • Maintain professionalism and empathy at all times.

Examples

“Welcome to the team — your role is to help customers feel confident, supported, and informed throughout their buying or selling journey.”
