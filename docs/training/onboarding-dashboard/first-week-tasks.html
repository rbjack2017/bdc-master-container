Overview

This module outlines the first week of onboarding: training modules, CRM practice, messaging practice, workflow review, and supervised lead handling.

Standards

  • Complete all onboarding modules by Day 5.
  • Shadow live calls and messages.
  • Begin handling leads with supervision by Day 3–4.
