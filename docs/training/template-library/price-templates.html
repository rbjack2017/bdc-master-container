Price Template Training

Learn how to send compliant, accurate, and effective price-related messages.

Training Notes

Price messaging must be compliant, accurate, and easy for customers to respond to. Avoid quoting payments, avoid unverifiable numbers, and always verify the vehicle before sending. Use one clear CTA and keep the tone helpful.

Short Version (AI‑Generated)

Hi {{First Name}}, I can send full pricing on the {{Vehicle}}. 
Do you prefer text or email?

Medium Version (Your Original)

Hi {{First Name}}, thanks for asking about pricing on the {{Vehicle}}. 
I can send a full breakdown — do you prefer text or email?

Long Version (AI‑Expanded)

Hi {{First Name}}, thank you for reaching out about pricing on the {{Vehicle}}. 
I can send a full, itemized breakdown including market comparison, dealership discounts, 
and any available incentives. Before I send it, do you prefer receiving the details 
by text or email?
