Price Template Training

Learn how to send compliant, accurate, and effective price-related messages.

Overview

Price templates must be accurate, compliant, and easy for customers to respond to.

How to Use Price Templates

  • Always verify the vehicle before sending.
  • Never quote payments or financing.
  • Keep the CTA simple and direct.
  • Match the customer’s tone.

Example Text Template

Hi {{First Name}}, thanks for asking about pricing on the {{Vehicle}}. 
I can send a full breakdown — do you prefer text or email?

Example Email Template

Subject: Pricing Details for Your {{Vehicle}}

Hi {{First Name}}, 
Thanks for reaching out about the {{Vehicle}}. I’ve attached a full price breakdown 
including market comparison and available options. 
Would you like me to include similar vehicles as well?

Example Voicemail Template

Hi {{First Name}}, this is Raven with Bachman. 
I received your request for pricing on the {{Vehicle}} and wanted to help with next steps. 
You can reach me at {{Phone}}.
