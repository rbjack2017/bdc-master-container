Trade Template Training

Learn how to request trade details and guide customers through valuation.

Training Notes

Trade templates must gather the right information without overwhelming the customer. Ask for the basics first, then follow up with additional details only if needed.

Short Version (AI‑Generated)

I can help with your trade — what’s the year, make, and model?

Medium Version (Your Original)

Hi {{First Name}}, I can help with your trade. 
What’s the year, make, and model of your vehicle?

Long Version (AI‑Expanded)

Hi {{First Name}}, I’d be happy to help with your trade-in. 
To get started, can you share the year, make, and model of your vehicle? 
Once I have that, I can walk you through the valuation process and let you know 
what additional details we may need.
