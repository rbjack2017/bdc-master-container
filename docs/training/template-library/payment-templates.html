Payment Template Training

Learn how to respond to payment questions without quoting or promising financing.

Training Notes

Payment questions must be handled carefully. Never quote payments, never imply approval, and never reference financing terms. Redirect the customer toward next steps that do not violate compliance.

Short Version (AI‑Generated)

I can help with payment options — are you planning to finance or pay cash?

Medium Version (Your Original)

Hi {{First Name}}, I can help with payment options on the {{Vehicle}}. 
Are you planning to finance or pay cash?

Long Version (AI‑Expanded)

Hi {{First Name}}, thanks for reaching out about payments on the {{Vehicle}}. 
I can walk you through the available options and help you compare what works best. 
To keep everything compliant and accurate, can you let me know whether you’re planning 
to finance or pay cash?
