QA Training Module

The QA Training Module explains how Quality Assurance scoring works, how reps are evaluated, and how QA impacts opportunity access, coaching, and certification.

1. Purpose of QA

  • Ensure communication quality
  • Maintain compliance standards
  • Improve customer experience
  • Identify coaching needs
  • Protect dealership reputation

2. QA Scoring Categories

Each communication is evaluated across several categories:

  • Compliance
  • Tone & Professionalism
  • Template Accuracy
  • Workflow Alignment
  • Customer Engagement
  • Documentation Quality

3. QA Score Thresholds

  • 70+: Fully eligible for opportunities
  • 60–69: Coaching required
  • 50–59: Restricted opportunity access
  • Below 50: Formal review

4. Common QA Deductions

  • Pricing in text messages
  • Missing CRM notes
  • Incorrect template usage
  • Unprofessional tone
  • Skipping workflow steps
  • Incorrect source logging

5. How to Improve QA Scores

  • Use approved templates
  • Follow workflow timing
  • Document everything
  • Maintain professional tone
  • Review coaching notes

6. QA Summary

QA ensures reps communicate clearly, professionally, and consistently. High QA scores lead to better opportunities, stronger performance, and advancement through certification levels.
