QA Training Module

The QA Training Module defines how communication quality is measured, how reps are scored, how violations are tracked, and how coaching is assigned. QA ensures consistency, compliance, professionalism, and workflow accuracy.

1. Purpose of QA

  • Ensure communication meets dealership standards
  • Protect compliance
  • Maintain professionalism
  • Identify coaching needs
  • Support certification advancement
  • Provide fair and consistent scoring

2. QA Scoring Categories

QA evaluates communication across six core categories.

1. Compliance

  • No pricing in text messages
  • No guarantees
  • No misleading statements
  • No credit discussions outside secure application
  • No emojis

2. Tone

  • Warm and professional
  • Clear and concise
  • No pressure
  • No negativity

3. Accuracy

  • Correct information
  • Correct templates
  • Correct workflow step
  • Correct customer status

4. Clarity

  • Easy to understand
  • Short sentences
  • One main question
  • Clear next step

5. Customization

  • Correct placeholders
  • Specific to customer
  • Removes irrelevant lines
  • Matches customer tone

6. Workflow Alignment

  • Correct timing
  • Correct task creation
  • Correct follow-up structure
  • Correct inactivation rules

3. QA Scoring Scale

QA scores range from 0–100 and determine coaching requirements.

90–100: Excellent

  • Fully compliant
  • Strong tone
  • Accurate and clear
  • Correct workflow

80–89: Good

  • Minor issues
  • Small clarity or customization errors

70–79: Needs Improvement

  • Multiple minor issues
  • Inconsistent workflow alignment
  • Coaching required

Below 70: Critical

  • Major compliance issues
  • Incorrect workflow
  • Incorrect templates
  • Immediate coaching required

4. QA Review Types

QA reviews communication across all channels.

Call Reviews

  • Tone
  • Accuracy
  • Compliance
  • Next steps

Email Reviews

  • Template accuracy
  • Clarity
  • Compliance
  • Customization

Text Reviews

  • Compliance
  • One-question rule
  • Clarity
  • Tone

CRM Note Reviews

  • Accuracy
  • Professionalism
  • Correct next task
  • Correct workflow step

5. QA Coaching System

QA scores determine coaching requirements.

Coaching Triggers

  • Score below 80
  • Repeated compliance issues
  • Incorrect workflow steps
  • Incorrect templates

Coaching Methods

  • Call reviews
  • Email reviews
  • Text reviews
  • Workflow audits
  • Roleplay sessions

6. QA Dashboard

The QA Dashboard tracks all communication quality metrics.

Dashboard Tracks:

  • QA scores
  • Compliance violations
  • Workflow violations
  • Template accuracy
  • Trend analysis
  • Monthly performance

7. QA Violations

Violations occur when reps break QA or compliance rules.

Minor Violations

  • Missing placeholders
  • Overly long messages
  • Multiple questions in a text
  • Incorrect tone

Major Violations

  • Pricing in text messages
  • Payment quotes in text messages
  • Guarantees
  • Misleading statements
  • Incorrect workflow
  • Unapproved templates

8. QA Summary

QA ensures communication is compliant, professional, accurate, and aligned with workflows. It protects the dealership and supports rep development through fair scoring and structured coaching.
