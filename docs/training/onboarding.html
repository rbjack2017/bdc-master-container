Onboarding Module

The Onboarding Module introduces new BDC reps to the dealership, the BDC Operating System, communication standards, workflows, and expectations. It ensures every rep starts with clarity, confidence, and the tools needed to succeed.

1. Onboarding Goals

  • Introduce reps to dealership culture
  • Explain BDC structure and responsibilities
  • Provide access to tools and systems
  • Train reps on communication standards
  • Prepare reps for certification

2. First Week Expectations

  • Complete Governance, CRM Tasks, and Workflows modules
  • Shadow experienced reps
  • Learn approved templates
  • Practice objection handling
  • Begin basic CRM navigation

3. Tools & Systems Overview

New reps must learn how to use all required systems:

  • CRM platform
  • Phone system
  • Email and text tools
  • Knowledge Base
  • Training Hub

4. Communication Standards

New reps must follow all communication rules from Day 1.

  • No emojis
  • No pricing in text messages
  • No guarantees
  • Use approved templates only
  • Maintain professional tone

5. Workflow Introduction

Reps learn the full Day 1–7 workflow structure.

  • Respond within 10 minutes
  • Complete tasks on time
  • Document everything
  • Inactivate on Day 7

6. Certification Path

New reps begin the BDCCR Level 1 certification track.

  • Complete foundational modules
  • Pass Level 1 knowledge check
  • Meet KPI benchmarks

7. Onboarding Summary

The Onboarding Module ensures new reps understand expectations, communication standards, workflows, and certification requirements. It sets the foundation for long‑term success in the BDC.
