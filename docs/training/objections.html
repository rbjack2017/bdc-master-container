Objections Module

The Objections Module teaches reps how to respond to customer concerns, questions, and hesitation using approved, compliant, and effective language.

1. Purpose of Objection Handling

  • Reduce customer hesitation
  • Increase appointment set rate
  • Maintain compliance and professionalism
  • Guide customers toward the next step

2. Common Objections

“I’m just looking.”

Customers often say this to avoid commitment. Use soft, helpful language.

  • “Totally understand — happy to help you look.”
  • “Is there a specific model you’re comparing?”

“Do you have the vehicle in stock?”

Always check inventory before answering.

  • “I can check availability — what color or trim do you prefer?”
  • “If it’s sold, I can find similar options.”

“What’s the price?”

Never quote pricing in text messages.

  • “I can connect you with a product specialist for exact pricing.”
  • “Are you comparing lease or purchase options?”

“I’m not ready yet.”

Use supportive, non‑pushy language.

  • “No rush — I’m here whenever you’re ready.”
  • “Is there anything you’d like to know in the meantime?”

3. Tone Rules

  • Never argue with the customer
  • Stay positive and helpful
  • Match customer tone (formal or casual)
  • Avoid emojis and slang

4. Low‑Effort Questions

These are simple questions customers ask that require quick, compliant answers.

  • “Hours?” → “We’re open until 8 PM today.”
  • “Location?” → “We’re at 9650 Bluegrass Pkwy.”
  • “Still available?” → “I can check — what trim are you looking for?”

5. Objection Summary

Objection handling helps reps guide customers confidently while maintaining compliance and professionalism.
