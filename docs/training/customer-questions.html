Customer Questions Library

1. Discovery – Needs & Preferences

  • What can I help you accomplish today?
  • What are you looking for in your next vehicle?
  • What are your must-have requirements?
  • What will you mainly be using the vehicle for?
  • How many passengers or how much space do you typically need?
  • What features are most important to you?
  • What have you seen so far that you’ve liked or didn’t like?
  • What’s prompting the change right now?
  • If everything went perfectly, what would your next vehicle do differently for you?
  • What do you wish your current vehicle did better?
  • Urgency & Consequence
  • What happens if you don’t make a change right now?
  • Is there anything pushing you to make a decision sooner rather than later?

2. Vehicle Fit & Options

  • Have you driven anything you’re considering?
  • Have you seen or experienced this vehicle in person yet?
  • Do you have a specific model, trim, or color in mind?
  • Are you open to similar options if I find something comparable?
  • If the exact vehicle isn’t available, what are you willing to be flexible on?
  • Are there any deal-breakers I should avoid?
  • Which vehicle are you most interested in?
  • Would you like to see a Carfax report?
  • What would make you feel confident long-term?
  • Is warranty coverage or maintenance history important?
  • Do you typically keep vehicles for a few years or long-term?
  • What does a typical day with your vehicle look like?
  • Are you ready to schedule a time to come in?
  • What stood out to you online?
  • Was anything missing from the listing?
  • Would photos, a video, or a comparison help?
  • Competitive Insight
  • What other vehicles are you comparing this to?
  • What stands out about those options?
  • How do you think this fits your daily routine?

3. Budget & Financing

  • Are you planning to pay cash, finance, or lease?
  • Did you have a down payment amount in mind?
  • Would you like to get pre-approved?
  • What feels like a comfortable investment?
  • How do you prefer to structure a purchase?
  • Do you mind if we talk briefly about budget?
  • Have you looked at pricing or payments yet?
  • Are you looking to purchase or lease?
  • How much would you like to put down?
  • What monthly payment range works for you?
  • Are you pre-approved or need financing help?

4. Trade-In

  • Are you considering trading in your current vehicle?
  • Do you have a vehicle to trade?
  • About how many miles are on it?
  • Is it paid off or financed?
  • Would you like a quick appraisal when you come in?

5. Appointment & Next Steps

  • Would you like to schedule a time to stop by?
  • What day and time works best?
  • How would you feel about taking it for a spin today?
  • Would you like to speak with a specialist first?
  • Is there anything I can have ready before you arrive?
  • How does this option feel compared to what you had in mind?
  • On a scale from 1–10, how close is this to what you’re looking for?
  • Want me to walk you through how this usually goes?
  • Is anything not feeling quite right yet?
  • If everything lined up, would you move forward?
  • What would make this worth the trip?
  • Would you prefer one best option or a few choices?

6. Communication & Follow-Up

  • What’s the best way to reach you?
  • What is your ZIP code?
  • What’s holding you back from coming in?
  • What would make this a great experience?
  • What concerns do you still have?
  • Is there anything you’re unsure about?
  • What would you need to feel 100% comfortable?
  • If you want to think on it, what’s the next step?

7. Decision Process

  • Besides yourself, is anyone else involved?
  • What does your decision timeline look like?
  • What’s most important when choosing where to buy?
  • What kind of experience are you hoping to have?
  • Are you researching or planning to purchase soon?

8. Emotional Connection / Personal Context

  • What do you enjoy most about driving?
  • What would make this vehicle feel perfect for you?
  • What kind of vehicle feels most like you?
  • What do you want your next car to say about you?
  • What would make you excited to get in your car daily?
  • What gives you peace of mind in a vehicle?
  • How would the right vehicle change your day?
  • What would this make easier for you or your family?

9. Phone Number Requests

  • What's the best number to reach you if I find a similar vehicle?
  • Would it be easier to text you a walkaround video?
  • If I locate additional options, what's the best number to send them to?
  • Would you prefer email only, text updates, or a quick call?
