Unavailable Redirect

How to redirect customers when a vehicle is temporarily unavailable, off‑lot, or in reconditioning.

Overview

Unavailable Redirect is used when a vehicle is not sold, but cannot be shown yet. This module teaches how to set expectations, offer alternatives, and keep customers engaged until the vehicle becomes available.

Standards

  • Explain the reason for unavailability clearly.
  • Provide an estimated timeline when possible.
  • Offer alternatives or a notification option.

Examples

“It’s currently in reconditioning, but I can notify you as soon as it’s ready. In the meantime, would you like to see similar options?”
