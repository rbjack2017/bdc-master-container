Sold Redirect

How to redirect customers when their vehicle of interest has sold — without losing the opportunity.

Overview

Sold Redirect is one of the most important continuity skills. This module teaches how to acknowledge the sold status clearly, discover must‑haves, and offer strong alternatives that keep the customer engaged.

Standards

  • Acknowledge sold status immediately.
  • Ask what mattered most about the original vehicle.
  • Offer 2–3 strong alternatives based on must‑haves.

Examples

“The {{Vehicle}} has sold, but I can help find similar options. What mattered most — price, features, mileage, or color?”
