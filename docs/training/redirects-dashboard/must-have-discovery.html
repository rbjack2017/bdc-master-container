Must‑Have Discovery

How to identify the customer’s must‑have features so you can redirect them accurately and confidently.

Overview

Must‑Have Discovery is the foundation of all redirect messaging. This module teaches how to ask simple questions that reveal what matters most, so you can match customers with the right alternatives.

Standards

  • Ask what mattered most about the original vehicle.
  • Clarify top priorities (price, mileage, features, color).
  • Use must‑haves to select alternatives.

Examples

“What mattered most about the {{Vehicle}} — price, features, mileage, or color?” “Are you flexible on trim level or do you prefer something specific?”
