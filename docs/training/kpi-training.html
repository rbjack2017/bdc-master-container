KPI Training Module

The KPI Training Module defines how BDC performance is measured, what metrics matter, how they are calculated, and how reps can improve. KPIs ensure accountability, consistency, and predictable results.

1. Purpose of KPIs

  • Measure performance accurately
  • Identify strengths and weaknesses
  • Support coaching and development
  • Ensure consistent customer experience
  • Drive predictable results
  • Provide transparency and fairness

2. Core KPIs

These KPIs define BDC performance standards.

1. Speed-to-Lead

  • Goal: Respond within 10 minutes
  • Measured by: CRM timestamp
  • Impact: Higher engagement and appointment rate

2. Contact Rate

  • Goal: 60–75%
  • Measured by: Successful conversations
  • Impact: More appointments and conversions

3. Appointment Set Rate

  • Goal: 35–50%
  • Measured by: Appointments scheduled
  • Impact: Higher showroom traffic

4. Appointment Show Rate

  • Goal: 60–75%
  • Measured by: Customers who arrive
  • Impact: More sales opportunities

5. Conversion Rate

  • Goal: 25–40%
  • Measured by: Sold units from appointments
  • Impact: Direct revenue impact

6. Workflow Accuracy

  • Goal: 100%
  • Measured by: CRM task completion and workflow adherence
  • Impact: Predictable customer experience

7. Compliance Score

  • Goal: 100%
  • Measured by: QA audits
  • Impact: Protects dealership reputation

3. KPI Definitions

Each KPI has a clear definition to ensure fairness and accuracy.

Speed-to-Lead

Time between lead creation and first contact attempt.

Contact Rate

Percentage of leads where a conversation occurred.

Appointment Set Rate

Percentage of leads where an appointment was scheduled.

Appointment Show Rate

Percentage of scheduled appointments that arrived.

Conversion Rate

Percentage of appointments that resulted in a sale.

Workflow Accuracy

Percentage of workflow steps completed correctly.

Compliance Score

Percentage of communication that meets compliance standards.

4. KPI Coaching

KPI coaching helps reps improve performance through targeted feedback.

Coaching Triggers

  • Speed-to-lead below 10 minutes
  • Contact rate below 60%
  • Appointment set rate below 35%
  • Show rate below 60%
  • Workflow accuracy below 100%
  • Compliance score below 100%

Coaching Methods

  • Call reviews
  • Email reviews
  • Text reviews
  • Workflow audits
  • Task audits
  • Roleplay sessions

5. KPI Dashboard

The KPI Dashboard tracks all performance metrics in real time.

Dashboard Includes:

  • Speed-to-lead
  • Contact rate
  • Appointment set rate
  • Appointment show rate
  • Conversion rate
  • Workflow accuracy
  • Compliance score
  • Trend analysis
  • Monthly performance

6. KPI Improvement Strategies

Reps can improve KPIs through consistent habits and workflow discipline.

  • Respond immediately to new leads
  • Use correct templates
  • Follow workflows exactly
  • Complete all tasks daily
  • Use low-effort questions in texts
  • Confirm appointments clearly
  • Send reminders
  • Log all communication

7. KPI Summary

KPI training ensures reps understand how performance is measured and how to improve. KPIs drive consistency, accountability, and predictable results across the BDC.
