KPI Training Module

The KPI Training Module explains the performance metrics used to evaluate BDC reps. KPIs ensure accountability, consistency, and measurable progress toward dealership goals.

1. Core KPIs

  • Response Time
  • Contact Rate
  • Appointment Set Rate
  • Appointment Show Rate
  • Sold Rate
  • Task Completion Rate

2. Response Time

Reps must respond to new opportunities within 10 minutes. Faster response times increase contact rates and customer satisfaction.

3. Contact Rate

Contact rate measures how many customers you successfully reach. Higher contact rates lead to more appointments and sales.

  • Use approved templates
  • Follow workflow timing
  • Attempt multiple contact methods

4. Appointment Set Rate

This KPI tracks how many contacted customers convert into appointments.

  • Use strong appointment-setting language
  • Offer multiple time options
  • Confirm details clearly

5. Appointment Show Rate

Shows how many scheduled appointments actually arrive at the dealership.

  • Send reminders
  • Confirm appointment details
  • Maintain professional tone

6. Sold Rate

Measures how many appointments convert into sales. This KPI reflects the quality of your communication and appointment setting.

7. Task Completion Rate

Tracks how consistently reps complete CRM tasks on time. High task completion ensures workflow accuracy and compliance.

8. KPI Summary

KPI performance determines coaching needs, opportunity eligibility, and overall BDC success.
