Workflows Module

The Workflows Module defines the exact steps reps must follow from Day 1 through Day 7. It ensures consistency, compliance, and proper customer engagement timing.

1. Day 1 Workflow

  • Respond within 10 minutes
  • Send Day 1 email with OEM-required elements
  • Complete Day 1 tasks immediately
  • Log all communication in CRM

2. Day 2–3 Workflow

  • Send follow-up email
  • Attempt phone contact
  • Complete scheduled CRM tasks
  • Document all attempts

3. Day 4–6 Workflow

  • Continue follow-up attempts
  • Use approved templates only
  • Maintain professional tone
  • Ensure all tasks are completed on time

4. Day 7 Workflow

  • Final follow-up attempt
  • Inactivate the opportunity
  • No tasks after inactivation

5. Workflow Summary

Workflows ensure reps follow a consistent, compliant, and effective communication pattern throughout the customer lifecycle.
