Workflows Module

The Workflows Module defines the exact daily follow-up structure for every customer, ensuring consistency, compliance, and predictable communication. Workflows protect the customer experience and prevent missed opportunities.

1. Purpose of Workflows

  • Ensure consistent follow-up timing
  • Provide a predictable customer experience
  • Support OEM compliance
  • Maintain CRM accuracy
  • Prevent missed opportunities
  • Guide reps through each day of follow-up

2. Workflow Overview

Every lead follows a structured 7-day workflow unless converted, closed, or redirected.

Day 1 Workflow

  • Immediate call attempt
  • OEM-compliant Day 1 email
  • One compliant text
  • Log all communication
  • Create Day 2 follow-up task

Day 2–6 Workflow

  • Daily call attempt
  • Daily email or text based on customer behavior
  • Log all communication
  • Create next-day follow-up task

Day 7 Workflow

  • Final call attempt
  • Final email
  • Inactivate lead
  • Correct dead note: “Due to Processes – Non-Responsive”
  • No tasks after inactivation

3. Workflow Communication Standards

All communication must follow compliance rules and be logged accurately.

Call Requirements

  • Professional tone
  • Clear purpose
  • No pressure
  • Accurate information

Email Requirements

  • OEM-compliant Day 1 email
  • Clear next steps
  • Professional tone
  • No misleading statements

Text Requirements

  • Short and easy to answer
  • One question only
  • No pricing
  • No payment quotes

4. Workflow Accuracy Requirements

Workflow accuracy is mandatory. Deviations cause customer confusion and missed opportunities.

  • Follow each day’s steps exactly
  • Use correct templates
  • Log all communication
  • Create correct next tasks
  • Inactivate on Day 7

5. Workflow Exceptions

Some situations require workflow adjustments.

Customer Responds

  • Switch to Response Handling workflow
  • Match communication to customer’s question
  • Adjust next task accordingly

Vehicle Sold

  • Switch to Redirect workflow
  • Send Sold Redirect message
  • Offer similar options

Appointment Set

  • Switch to Appointment workflow
  • Confirm appointment
  • Send reminder

6. Workflow Compliance

Workflows must follow compliance rules at all times.

  • No pricing in text messages
  • No guarantees
  • No misleading statements
  • No skipping workflow steps

7. Workflow Summary

Workflows ensure consistent follow-up, protect the customer experience, and maintain compliance. Every rep must follow workflows exactly to ensure predictable and professional communication.
