Templates Library

The Templates Library contains all approved email, text, and voicemail templates used in daily BDC communication. These templates ensure consistency, compliance, clarity, and ease of use.

1. Purpose of Templates

  • Ensure consistent communication
  • Protect compliance
  • Reduce writing time
  • Increase response rates
  • Support workflow accuracy
  • Provide clear next steps for customers

2. Template Rules

General Rules

  • Use templates exactly as written
  • Customize only approved fields
  • Remove anything that does not apply
  • Ask one main question
  • Match the customer’s tone
  • End with one clear next step

Compliance Rules

  • No pricing in text messages
  • No payment quotes
  • No guarantees (“approval,” “rate,” “value”)
  • No misleading statements
  • No emojis

3. Email Templates

New Lead Email

Subject: Thanks for your interest in the {{Vehicle}}

Hi {{First Name}}, thanks for reaching out about the {{Vehicle}}. I can help with availability, details, and next steps. What matters most to you — price, mileage, features, or timing?

Day 1 OEM-Compliant Email

Hi {{First Name}}, thanks for your interest in the {{Vehicle}}. I’m here to help with details, availability, and next steps. If you prefer, I can also send similar options or incoming units.

Price Request Email

Subject: Quick Questions for Your Price Request

Hi {{First Name}}, I can help with accurate pricing for the {{Vehicle}}. To make sure it’s correct, could you confirm your ZIP code, trade status, and whether you prefer finance, lease, or cash?

Payment Request Email

Subject: Quick Questions for Your Payment Estimate

Hi {{First Name}}, I can help put together a payment estimate. Could you confirm your ZIP code, down payment (if any), and whether you prefer finance or lease?

Trade Value Email

Subject: Quick Questions for Your Trade Value

Hi {{First Name}}, I can help get your trade value. Could you send the year, make, model, mileage, payoff, and VIN?

Appointment Confirmation Email

Subject: Appointment Confirmation

Hi {{First Name}}, I have you scheduled for {{Appointment Time}}. I’ll have the {{Vehicle}} ready for you. If anything changes, just let me know.

No-Show Email

Subject: Sorry We Missed You

Hi {{First Name}}, I’m sorry we missed you for your appointment. If you’re still interested, I can help you reschedule for {{Option 1}} or {{Option 2}}.

Sold Redirect Email

Subject: Update on the {{Vehicle}}

Hi {{First Name}}, the {{Vehicle}} has sold, but I can help find similar options if you’re open to that. What matters most — price, mileage, color, trim, or features?

6. Customization Rules

Before sending any template, make it specific, short, and easy to answer.

  • Replace placeholders
  • Remove anything that doesn’t apply
  • Ask one main question
  • Match the customer’s tone
  • End with one clear next step

7. Template Violations

Minor Violations

  • Missing placeholders
  • Overly long messages
  • Multiple questions

Major Violations

  • Pricing in text messages
  • Guarantees
  • Misleading statements
  • Unapproved templates

8. Templates Summary

Templates ensure consistent, compliant, and effective communication. They reduce writing time, increase response rates, and support workflow accuracy.
