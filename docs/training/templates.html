Templates Module

The Templates Module provides approved email, text, and voicemail scripts for BDC reps. These templates ensure consistency, compliance, and professionalism across all customer communication.

1. Email Templates

New Lead Email

  • Thank the customer for their interest
  • Confirm the vehicle of interest
  • Ask one simple question
  • Invite them to continue the conversation

Follow-Up Email

  • Reference previous communication
  • Ask a clarifying question
  • Offer assistance

2. Text Templates

All text messages must be short, compliant, and easy to answer.

  • “Hi {{First Name}}, this is Raven with Bachman. Are you still considering the {{Vehicle}}?”
  • “Any questions I can help with?”
  • “Would you like to schedule a visit?”

3. Voicemail Templates

Voicemails must be clear, professional, and under 20 seconds.

  • “Hi {{First Name}}, this is Raven with Bachman. I tried reaching you and will send a quick text as well. Talk soon.”

4. Customization Rules

  • Replace placeholders
  • Remove irrelevant lines
  • Ask one main question
  • Match customer tone

5. Template Summary

Templates ensure reps communicate clearly, professionally, and consistently while maintaining compliance.
