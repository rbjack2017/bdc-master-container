BDC Workflows

New Lead Workflow
  • Step 1: First response (email + text + voicemail)
  • Step 2: Follow-up within 2 hours
  • Step 3: Day 2 re-engage
  • Step 4: Day 3–7 alternating follow-up attempts
Appointment Workflow
  • Step 1: Confirm appointment time + date
  • Step 2: Send reminder text
  • Step 3: Prepare vehicle before arrival
  • Step 4: Notify salesperson
Price Workflow
  • Step 1: Send price email + text
  • Step 2: Clarify budget + timeline
  • Step 3: Offer similar options if needed
  • Step 4: Follow-up next day
Trade Workflow
  • Step 1: Gather trade details (VIN, miles, photos)
  • Step 2: Set expectation on appraisal
  • Step 3: Schedule appraisal or visit
  • Step 4: Follow-up with value range
Quiet Lead Workflow
  • Step 1: Soft nudge text
  • Step 2: Alternate channel attempt
  • Step 3: Offer help or alternatives
  • Step 4: Close out after 7 days
Sold Redirect Workflow
  • Step 1: Notify customer vehicle sold
  • Step 2: Offer similar alternatives
  • Step 3: Ask about flexibility (color, trim, mileage)
  • Step 4: Send options
Daily Workflow (Day 1–7)
  • Day 1: First response + voicemail + text
  • Day 2: Quiet lead text
  • Day 3: Alternate channel attempt
  • Day 4: Price or trade follow-up
  • Day 5: Soft nudge
  • Day 6: Send options or video
  • Day 7: Final follow-up
Long-Term Follow-Up (30 / 60 / 90 Days)
  • 30-Day: Check-in + offer help
  • 60-Day: Provide updated inventory
  • 90-Day: Final follow-up + close file
