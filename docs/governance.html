BDC Enterprise Governance System

Governance Purpose & Executive Outcome
  • Workflow consistency
  • Messaging consistency
  • KPI consistency
  • Reporting consistency
  • Leadership consistency
  • Compliance consistency
  • Culture consistency
  • Outcome: A BDC that operates like a corporate division, not a department.
The Six Pillars of Enterprise Governance
  • Policy Governance: Controls rules, workflows, communication standards.
  • Performance Governance: Controls KPIs at all levels.
  • Reporting Governance: Controls cadence and visibility.
  • Leadership Governance: Controls coaching, culture, accountability.
  • Compliance Governance: Controls legal and ethical standards.
  • Risk Governance: Controls crisis and disruption response.

Governance Cadence
  • Daily: KPI visibility, workflow accuracy, coaching touchpoints.
  • Weekly: Manager scorecards, workflow audits, ROI checks.
  • Monthly: Store scorecards, staffing review, compensation review.
  • Quarterly: Executive war room, enterprise audits.
  • Annual: Budget, staffing, compensation, marketing, expansion.
Roles & Responsibilities
  • BDC Rep: Workflow accuracy, template accuracy, KPI ownership.
  • Senior Rep: Mentorship, quality control.
  • Team Lead: Daily coaching, KPI monitoring.
  • BDC Manager: Weekly coaching, scorecards, compliance oversight.
  • BDC Director: Multi-store alignment, enterprise KPIs.
  • GM/Owner: Budget, staffing, compensation, marketing.
  • Executive Team: Governance oversight, long-term planning.
